Still check by hand

  • Bold class members’ names on first mention. The checker catches alumni written with a class year and names bolded later but not the first time. A classmate mentioned only by name needs your eye.
  • Spelling of names and places.
  • Place names include a state, province or country (major cities stand alone).
  • Titles in quotes, not italics; newspapers and magazines in neither.
  • Tone, unclear names and sensitive news.
